# Demo Material

These scripts are recording plans, not evidence of completed live acceptance.
Use a freshly approved amount and label the recording as an Arc Testnet demo;
illustrative 250 USDC examples do not authorize a payment or email.

Show only the payment-link domain. Redact bearer tokens and usable QR codes
from browser bars, connector output, screenshots, and recordings, and use
synthetic billing profiles. Billing contents stay offchain and are accessible
to authorized workspace users and anyone holding the bearer link. Settlement
terms, addresses, invoice key, and document commitment are public onchain.

## Architecture Reference

The user-owned `../architecture.excalidraw.svg` is preserved unchanged as a
historical concept, not the current implemented architecture. Financing,
liquidity pools, credit scoring, auditing, tokenized collateral, and quoted
rates are illustrative only and are not available product features. The
financing arithmetic has not been validated and must not be used as financial
guidance. Email delivery remains operator-gated; unattended production
delivery is not proven. Do not submit this diagram as the final architecture
without the owner's review and correction of both visible and embedded source.
