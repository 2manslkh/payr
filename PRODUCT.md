## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Payr serves independent developers and their invoiced clients. The confirmed product charter and source precedence remain in `PROJECT.md`; this design-tool context does not replace them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The user confirmed that R08 extends the protected-document flow with a receipt showing exact settled amount, invoice reference, payer/payee, transaction/block evidence, hashes, QR, and PDF download. Contact addresses and delivery-recipient details stay private. Receipt and delivery completion must be supported by verified artifacts and provider evidence, not inferred from payment submission.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the approved Commit Ledger system in `DESIGN.md`, Payr capitalization and existing artwork. The receipt has no dashboard chrome and is not a product redesign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve WCAG AA contrast, semantic status text, visible keyboard focus, desktop/mobile layouts, 44px controls, and reduced-motion support.
